## Theme

Falling notes are fictional **werewolf enemies**. There is no visible guardian,
hero, player avatar, or friendly companion character in the release art.

## Exclusion summary

Original prototype media was excluded from the release. Automated checks
confirmed that no original photographs, background artwork, or unapproved
audio files are included.

Prototype photographs and the prototype background illustration are **not**
distributed. They were used only as local visual references while designing
fictional anthropomorphic werewolf enemies. No photograph pixels were embedded
or copied. Exact tattoos and other uniquely identifying details were omitted.
The real person’s name is not included.

### Shared enemy design brief

Masculine adult chibi werewolf: charcoal-gray fur; lighter muzzle/chest/abdomen;
black beanie; optional rectangular glasses; darker lower-muzzle fur (beard
impression only); broad shoulders; stylized abs; dark athletic shorts;
challenging / mischievous enemy expression; transparent PNG; readable at note
size.

### Generation prompts (summary)

Base: commercial-quality stylized chibi anthropomorphic werewolf enemy for an
indie rhythm game; charcoal-gray fur; silver-gray muzzle/chest/abdomen; black
knit beanie; optional rectangular black glasses; darker lower-muzzle fur;
expressive wolf eyebrows; small cartoon fangs; broad shoulders; muscular arms;
stylized six-pack; dark athletic shorts; large head; compact Q-body; cel
shading; transparent/light key background; no text/logo.

Negative: no human skin; no realistic human face; no photorealism; no exact
portrait recreation; no tattoos; no phone/mirror/bathroom; no blood/gore;
no weapons; no franchise mascots; no watermark.

Variants differ only by expression/pose while keeping the same pack identity.

Background: moonlit village edge, stylized forest, distant wolf silhouettes,
soft mountains, calm tense night mood, open readable space for menu text;
no PEACE/平静 text; no gore.
